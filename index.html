    Controls

    Aim
    Move the mouse, or tap where you want the shot to go.
    Shoot
    Left click or tap.
    Scope
    Hold Shift, or press Z to toggle. It shows the range.
    Reload
    Right click, Space, R, or the Reload button.
    Help
    H or the Help button. The hunt pauses while this is open. Esc closes it.

    Ballistics

    Zero
    The rifle is zeroed at 100 yd. It hits 4 inches low at 200 and 14 low at 300. The scope has hold marks for both.
    Flight
    The round lands where the crosshair is when you fire. No lead needed.
    Recoil
    The muzzle climbs after each shot. Let it settle.
    Timber
    Trunks stop bullets. Wait for the gap.

    Scoring

    Rack
    100 per antler point, plus 2 points per yard of range.
    Placement
    Heart-lung +500, head +250, neck +150. A gut shot only wounds.
    Extras
    Quick draw +200, running shot +250, clean hunt +500.
    Does
    Any hit on a doe costs 500. The site keeps going.
